# Content plan: 10 months, one video a week

Week 1 starts **Mon 23 Nov 2026**, and the plan runs through mid-September 2027 (43 videos).
Some topics are timed to the calendar: New Year (habits), Valentine's Day (love), exam season (study) and back-to-school.
Every video is still built the same way: a relatable cold open, 5–7 chapters, named studies on screen, sources in the description, 8:00+ long.

**Mix rule:** alternate the categories so the channel doesn't feel repetitive:
everyday struggles 🧠 · relationships ❤️ · famous experiments & myths 🔬 · the "dark side" 🌑 · feel-good 🌞

- Include a "talk to a professional" card.
- Never encourage armchair diagnosis.
- Include help resources in the description where relevant.
