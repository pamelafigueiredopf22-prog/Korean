## Short description (draft)
50 big, friendly word searches with large, easy-to-read letters. Every puzzle has a fun theme
(farm animals, pizza party, rocket launch, coral reef, snowy day and more) and a word list
with tick boxes. Arrows on each page show which way the words run: across and down at first,
then slanting words as kids get better. Answers are at the back.
