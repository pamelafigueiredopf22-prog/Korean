## Short description (draft)
Big paths, thick lines and friendly pictures make these 60 mazes just right for little hands.
Each maze is a mini adventure (help the bee find the flower, fly the rocket to the moon) and
the mazes grow a little harder as your child's confidence grows. Star ratings show the level,
